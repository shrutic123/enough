## Day 4 - Nov 12
- Done: Diagnosed the production trim failure on a 757-character prose draft. It was not the key and it was not a blanket quota wall: the request hit the 25-second timeout, and the fallback deliberately skipped timeouts, so the fast model was never tried. Measured the models directly on the same free key: `gemini-3.6-flash` took **62 seconds**, `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` **1.5 seconds**, and `gemini-3.5-flash` **0.8 seconds**. Switched the default to a fast model, capped each attempt at 12 seconds, and made a timeout trigger the fallback. Then hit the other free-tier trap: Flash is limited to about **20 requests a day**, which repeated testing exhausts, while Flash-Lite allows about **500**. Defaulted to `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` with `gemini-3.5-flash` as the fallback, and made a 429 report "The trim service is busy right now" instead of a generic failure. Measured Lite from 0.8 to 7.6 seconds on the same draft, so the cap has to allow for the slow end. Verified the sample locally in about a second with a clean trim; the previously failing emoji, long, injection and filler drafts all return 200. No billing is needed.
- Stuck on: Nothing blocking; deploy and re-check production.
- Next: Record the demo video, then the submission package.
